From booking to the last glowHow it works

How It WorksEasy from start to finish.

A simple 5-step process from start to finish.

  1. Check Your Date

    Enter your event date and quantity to check live availability and get a quote.

  2. Book Online

    Secure your date with a 50% deposit online. Remaining hire and bond are due 7 days before pickup.

  3. Pick Up

    Collect your pre-filled, securely packed candles from Bull Creek the day before your event, in your selected pickup window.

  4. Style & Enjoy

    Arrange and light them however you like. You’re in charge of the styling; the candle prep is done.

  5. Return

    Blow them out, repack in the original packaging and return the next day in your selected return window. We handle the cleaning.
