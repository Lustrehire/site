What happens next

1

Watch for your confirmation email

Your confirmation email will include your booking details, pickup address, and any final collection notes.

2

Keep your booking details handy

Your event date, candle quantity, and recorded time windows are all linked to this booking and will be confirmed by email.

3

Need to update anything?

If you need to adjust a detail, reply to your confirmation email and we can help from there.

Pickup address details are shared in your confirmation email. If you do not see it, please check your spam or promotions folder.
